In the items app (the repository `aaas-app-demo`): we want to see what matters
most first.

- A list of open items ordered by priority: priority 1 is the most important and
  comes first, then 2, and so on to 5. Items without a priority come after all
  items that have one.
- Items with the same priority (or both without one) are listed newest first.
- Done items are not in this list.
- It must be possible to limit the list to items at or above a given priority,
  for example "priority 2 or more important" gives only priorities 1 and 2.

built and how you know it works.
